    How it works

    Every 15 minutes NukeWatch reads world news (GDELT), U.S. State Department advisories, military flight feeds and social media. Headlines place each flashpoint on the ladder. Other signals are compared with their own recent history and down-weighted during known exercises.

    What it can’t see

    Launch orders, alert levels and most submarine movements are classified. Many military aircraft fly with transponders off. Treat this as context, not a warning system.
